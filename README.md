# Sparlex — Customer Feedback Automation

## Project Overview

Sparlex Customer Feedback Automation is an AI-assisted workflow designed to organize customer feedback and classify customer experiences using Zapier, Google Forms, and Gmail.

The goal is to simplify feedback processing, reduce repetitive manual work, and help a business understand whether customers had a good, medium, or bad experience.

## Project Objectives

- Automate the handling of customer feedback.
- Classify customer experiences as **Good**, **Medium**, or **Bad**.
- Connect Google Forms with other applications through Zapier.
- Support timely email communication using Gmail.
- Help the business identify customer satisfaction issues and opportunities for improvement.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Google Forms** — Collects customer feedback.
- **Zapier** — Connects applications and coordinates the automation workflow.
- **Gmail** — Supports email communication within the workflow.
- **AI-assisted classification** — Intended to help categorize customer feedback by experience level, if configured in the workflow.

## Workflow Overview

1. **Customer Feedback Submission:** A customer submits feedback through Google Forms.
2. **Zapier Trigger:** The workflow is designed to receive the submitted form response.
3. **Customer Experience Classification:** The intended process categorizes the customer's experience as Good, Medium, or Bad based on their feedback.
4. **Email Integration:** Gmail is included in the workflow to support the intended feedback communication process.
